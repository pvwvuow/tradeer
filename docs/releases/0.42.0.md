# 0.42.0

Telegram channels, part one: connect your own account and let the app read your "AI Lab" folder (docs/SIGNAL_DESK.md 3.1 to 3.3, phase 21c).

- Settings > Telegram channels: enter api_id and api_hash from my.telegram.org (the page shows the 3 steps), choose the proxy (the system proxy by default, so your VPN works; SOCKS5, HTTP and MTProto too) and log in with your phone number, the code Telegram sends and your two-step password.
- In Telegram, make a chat folder named "AI Lab" and add the signal channels to it. The page lists them; every new channel starts off, turn on the ones you want.
- For the channels that are on, every new message, edit and deletion is stored on this PC only (never in the cloud). The first text of a message is kept even when the channel edits or deletes it later.
- Read only: the app never posts, reacts, joins, leaves or marks anything as read. No signal cards from channels yet (next version), and no order ever goes out without your hold-to-confirm.
- The api_hash, the Telegram session and a proxy password stay in Windows Credential Manager, never in a file or a log. Log out ends the session on Telegram's side too.
- The Telegram library (Telethon 1.44.0) is part of the app now; nothing extra to install.
