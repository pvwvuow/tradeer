# 0.41.0

The Signal desk's AI note (docs/SIGNAL_DESK.md 2.5, phase 21b3).

- When the AI connection is on (AI settings, the gear), a pasted signal's card gets a short note after the full check: three sentences on what supports the trade, what speaks against it, and the biggest risk.
- The AI sees only what the card already shows: the plan, the checks, the context now and the full check. No account number, no lots, no money, no history.
- It may not add numbers, forecast the price or tell you to buy, sell or skip, and it has no way to send an order: only your hold does.
- One note per card, at most 300 tokens. With the AI off, the card simply has no note.
