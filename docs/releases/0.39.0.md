# 0.39.0

The Signal desk's full check (docs/SIGNAL_DESK.md 2.5, phase 21b1).

- When a pasted signal's card waits for you, the app checks the same geometry in the symbol's own history: on the last 2 years of M15 bars, how often a trade with the same side, the same stop loss and target distances (in ATR) and the same session reached each target before the stop loss.
- The rules are strict: when the stop loss and the target are both inside one bar, the stop loss counts first, and the spread is paid. One sample per hour, followed for at most 4 days.
- Every number shows its sample count. Under 30 samples the card says "too little data" and shows no percent. It is a base rate of the geometry, not a forecast of this trade, and the card says so.
- The card also shows how your pasted signals did so far.
- The check runs in the background with the Backtest page's history cache (the first one on a symbol can take a while), one at a time. Full check on the card runs it again.
