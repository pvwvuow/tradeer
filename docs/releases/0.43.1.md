# 0.43.1: the AI Lab chat can find problems and do more (10 October 2026)

## What is new

- **App doctor.** Ask the chat "what is wrong?", "why does nothing happen?" or "check the
  app". It checks the operating mode, the strategies that are on, a risk pause, the kill
  switch, the Telegram reader and channels, why signals were stopped, and the warnings and
  errors of the last 24 hours. Each known problem comes back in plain words with the one
  thing to do. Examples: MT5's Algo Trading is off, the Telegram bot cannot reach Telegram
  (VPN), not enough margin, too little price history, a channel in Paper trial.
- **The chat reads more:** every Telegram channel (on or off, Paper trial or Live, budget,
  stats, the last signals and what the app did with each one, the last messages read), the
  open bot trades, the account's risk limits and a guide to where everything is in the app.
- **The chat can propose changes.** "Give gg 100 dollars and set it to Live", "turn on
  ema_momentum", "close my gold trade", "move the stop to the entry". Each one becomes a
  card under the answer with **Hold to apply**. Nothing changes before the hold, and the
  card refuses when the values changed in the meantime.
  - Channel settings, turning a strategy off, closing a bot trade and a tighter stop loss
    are always possible.
  - Turning a strategy on, its parameters and the signal filters change only in Paper or
    Analysis-only mode, like an AI suggestion.
  - Never possible from the chat: new orders (paste the signal: the Signal desk makes an
    order card), the operating mode, Go-Live, the kill switch, the account's risk limits,
    passwords and keys.
- **A channel signal in Paper trial shows one line in the chat** ("XAUUSD signal read, no
  order card: Paper trial ..."), so you see it was read.

## To start

Open the AI Lab and ask in your own words, in Persian or English. To test a channel, post
a signal with today's prices (gold is near 4190, not 2361), with the stop loss on the losing
side.
