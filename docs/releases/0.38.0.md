# 0.38.0

The Signal desk (docs/SIGNAL_DESK.md, phase 21a): paste a trade signal into the AI Lab and get an order card.

- Paste a signal such as "XAUUSD sell limit 2361 SL 2368 TP 2355 TP 2348" (Persian words and digits work too). It becomes an order card instead of an AI question. Questions like "why did my EURUSD buy lose?" still go to the AI.
- The app plans it against the live bid and ask: market, limit or stop, the entry within reach of the price, the stop loss on the losing side, every target on the winning side, the stops level and the market hours.
- One position per target (legs). The whole signal is sized once by the risk manager and split over the legs, so together they risk what one trade may. Every filter and risk limit runs on every leg, and the decision trace is under Why?.
- Nothing is sent until you press and hold Hold to send: 1.2 s, or 2 s with a REAL ORDER warning when the order would be real. Letting go early cancels. The engine checks the price, the spread and the limits again before it sends. Skip dismisses the card. Auto mode never sends a pasted signal by itself, and the AI never sends an order.
- A signal without a stop loss, on a symbol that is not on the watchlist, or with prices on the wrong side is refused, and the card says why.
- Pasted signals use their own strategy, manual_signal (magic 26070007), so their results stay apart in every report.
- The AI Lab header shows the trading mode (PAPER, SEMI-AUTO, AUTO) and REAL ORDERS: ONLY WITH YOUR CONFIRM on a real account.
