# 0.36.0

- New look, step 4 of the No Curve v2 design (docs/NOCURVE_V2.md): the Simple view is drawn
  like the design, with real data only.
- One proposal at a time, PROPOSAL n/N with a LIVE or WAITING FOR DATA tag:
  - the symbol, the side and the strategy;
  - one sentence with the size and the money at stake on both sides;
  - the risk bar: the hatched loss up to the entry line, the profit after it, R:R below;
  - the win chance as 100 dots (low end, estimate, high end) with its range and sample
    count; a dash while there are too few past results;
  - WHY with real reasons only; Show details keeps the exact numbers.
- Hold to approve (mouse, Space or Enter; letting go early cancels), then the usual
  question; Skip next to it.
- Side column 372 px: open trades with their results and Close now, the day's result, the
  balance and the last 7 days.
- EMERGENCY: open the hatched cover first, then the same kill switch as the Advanced view
  (it still asks). Cancel closes the cover. The stop stays on screen on the first start too.
- A short message at the bottom says what an action did.
- Next: the AI Lab exactly like the design (20e).
