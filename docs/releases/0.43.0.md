# 0.43.0

Telegram channels, part two: a channel's signals become order cards in the AI Lab, each channel with its own budget (docs/SIGNAL_DESK.md 3.3 to 3.5, phase 21d1).

- Settings > Telegram channels: pick a channel in the list to set its mode, budget, risk per trade (2% of the channel's equity by default), max open trades (2), daily loss stop (5%), drawdown stop (30% of the budget), the symbols it may trade and its aliases (GOLD=XAUUSD). Save channel.
- A channel you turn on starts in **Paper trial**: its signals are read and counted, no card asks for money. Set it to **Live** with a budget and each new signal of that channel becomes an order card in the AI Lab chat, with the channel's name on it.
- A channel's card is a quick card (the full check runs when you press it) and waits for the same hold-to-confirm as a pasted one. Nothing is ever sent without your hold, and a channel's text can never change that.
- Every channel has its own magic number (26071001, 26071002, ...), so its trades, limits and results stay apart from the strategies, from pasted signals and from the other channels.
- Sizing with the channel's money: a trade risks the smaller of the account's risk per trade and the channel's (its risk % of its budget plus what its closed trades made). When even 0.01 lot would risk more than that, the card says so with both numbers and the trade is refused: raise the budget or the risk. The account's own limits always apply on top.
- The daily loss stop pauses the channel's cards for the day; the drawdown stop puts it back into Paper trial. A channel with its limit of open trades gets no new card until one closes.

Also in this version (phases 21d2 and 21e):

- **Shadow results for every signal**: every parsed signal of an on channel (also the ones you skip and every Paper trial signal) is followed on the price history from the message time plus the latency, with the spread paid and the stop loss first when both are in one bar. A channel's "close", "cancel" and "move SL to entry" count too.
- **Paper trial result**: after 20 resolved signals or 14 days the channel's settings show the result, and you can set it to Live.
- **Settings in the chat**: "give Gold Room 100 dollars", "به کانال Gold Room ۱۰۰ دلار بده", "set Gold Room risk to 1%" or "Gold Room live" shows a settings card with the old and new value; it is saved only after you hold the button.
- **Follow-ups**: "close now", "cancel", "move SL to entry" or "new SL 2340" from a channel, for a signal you took, shows a small card under it. One hold skips the orders that still wait, closes or cancels the bot trades, or moves their stop loss (never to a wider stop).
- **Duplicates**: the same signal from a second channel within 15 minutes joins the first card (both names on it) instead of making a new one; it is booked to the first channel.
- **Stats and ranking**: per channel signals, taken, skipped, refused by reason, win rate, average R, profit in money and % of the budget, max drawdown, profit factor, latency, slippage, deleted signals and edits after the result, plus the shadow results; the channels are ranked by the shadow average R once they have 20 resolved signals.
