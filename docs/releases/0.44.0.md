# 0.44.0: the AI Lab understands every kind of channel message

Channels often post a signal in pieces: "get ready", then "XAUUSD sell", then the stop and
targets a minute later. Some edit the first message, some write in their own style, some post
a picture. Before this build each piece was judged alone, so the app skipped them all.

## Half signals wait for their rest

- A channel message that is only a teaser ("صبر کنید", "get ready", "soon") or only part of a
  signal now opens a draft for that channel. The next pieces (new messages, replies or edits)
  are joined to it.
- When the joined text is a full signal, it goes through the normal path: Paper trial counts it,
  Live makes an order card that waits for your hold. Nothing is sent by itself.
- A draft lives 15 minutes. If it never gets complete, the app writes why in the log and, when it
  had a symbol or more than one piece, shows a short note in the AI Lab chat.
- Edited messages are read again. An edit that completes a signal is treated like a new piece.

## The AI reads what the parser cannot

- A message with a symbol (or a side) and three prices that is still no whole signal, like
  "Gold short from 4190, invalidation above 4200, targets 4170/4150", goes once to the AI with
  the channel's last 5 messages. So does a draft that stayed incomplete.
- The AI answers with JSON. Its reading counts only when every check passes: a symbol on the
  watchlist, a side, a stop loss and a target on the right sides of the entry, and every number
  written in the channel's messages (the AI may not invent one). Then it is a normal signal.
- At most 40 readings a day; the `channels` tool of the AI Lab says how many are left.
- A result message ("+120 pips today", "سود امروز") is the channel's own claim. It is never a
  signal and never costs an AI call.

## Signals posted as pictures

- The reader downloads the picture of a new message (only for channels that are on, at most
  2 MB, kept in memory, never stored). A picture without a whole signal in its text goes to the
  AI with the picture.
- This needs a model that reads images. The numbers of a picture cannot be checked against the
  text, so the chat note says "check the numbers on the card"; the sides are still checked and
  the card still waits for your hold.

## A softer AI Lab

- Rounder shapes in the AI Lab only: 14 px corners on cards, 10 px on buttons and fields, a
  round question bubble and pill-shaped quick prompts, a little more space between messages.
- The four welcome suggestions are now tiles in a 2 by 2 grid.
- Every AI answer has a small AI mark in front of it; the steps of an answer read as a timeline.
- New messages and cards fade in (not with reduced motion), then cards get a faint shadow.
- Colour still only where it means something. The other pages keep the sharp No Curve v2 look.
