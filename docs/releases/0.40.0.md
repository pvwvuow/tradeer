# 0.40.0

The Signal desk's context lines (docs/SIGNAL_DESK.md 2.5, phase 21b2).

- A pasted signal's card now shows "Context now": what the app already sees around the trade.
- The market watch's analysis card of the symbol: its headline (trend, level, volatility, session, verdict), the trend per timeframe with the bias, and the H1 structure.
- The spread now against its normal for this hour.
- The next high-impact news for either currency of the symbol.
- The bot's open trades on the same currencies, and which of them are the same bet (for example a EURUSD buy and a GBPUSD buy are both short USD).
- The lines come with the full check (by itself when the card waits, or with Full check). Nothing here sends an order: the hold is still the only way.
