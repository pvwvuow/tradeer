# 0.35.0

- New look, step 3 of the No Curve v2 design (docs/NOCURVE_V2.md): the Dashboard is drawn
  like the design, with real data only.
- Title row: the trading mode PAPER, SEMI-AUTO, AUTO, ANALYSIS shows the mode in use. AUTO
  wears a lock while Go-Live does not allow it. A click opens Positions, where the mode is
  changed with its checks.
- Four figures:
  - equity (with the mode), today, the win rate of the last 30 days with its 95% range and
    trade count, and the drawdown against its cap;
  - each with its arrow and a small line.
- Equity curve 170 px with the DRAWDOWN band below it.
- Open positions in the design's 7 columns:
  - symbol, side, lots, entry, the live price (LAST, from the market), the stop on the
    server with a lock, and the result;
  - strategy, TP and mode are in the tooltip.
- Signal decision trace in 56 px rows; the five steps carry their names below them.
- Side column 332 px:
  - sessions on a UTC scale;
  - the risk limits in the design's order: open risk, open trades, daily drawdown, size per
    trade against the 1% cap;
  - system health with lights;
  - the new Go-Live checklist: the 7 gate checks over every strategy that is on, then the
    typed approval;
  - the market direction at the bottom.
- Next: the Simple view exactly like the design (20d).
