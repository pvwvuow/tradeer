# Persian and right to left (Phase 16a)

Spec A ("UI language English + Persian (RTL, Vazirmatn font)"), F1 and G3 phase 16.

## Use it

Press **فارسی** in the top bar (or Advanced > Ctrl+K > "Change the language"). The choice is
saved in `<profile>/ui_prefs.json` (`"language": "fa"`) and used from the next start: close
the app and open it again. Press **English** to go back.

## What is Persian

- The Simple view: the first-start screen, Home (balance strip, the suggestion card and its
  details, open trades, the status line, Stop trading now) and its questions.
- The top bar: name, Settings, Simple/Advanced, theme, the MT5 and mode chips.
- The window runs right to left in the Vazirmatn font (bundled; Segoe UI or Tahoma when it is
  missing, for example in a source run).

The Advanced pages, Settings and the status bar stay English and left to right in this
version, so the trading terms there keep their usual names. Numbers, prices and symbols stay
in Latin digits (`+$12.30`, `XAUUSD`) so they read the same as in MetaTrader 5.

## How it works

`app/ui/home_model.py` still builds every text in English (the zero-jargon test reads it).
`app/ui/i18n.py` has a `Translator` that turns each finished text into Persian with the
templates of `app/ui/i18n_fa.py`, for example
`"You could lose about {amount} if it goes wrong"`. A field's value is translated again, so
"Gold (XAUUSD)" inside a sentence becomes "طلا (XAUUSD)". A text without a template stays as
it is. A new text on Home needs one line in `i18n_fa.py`; `tests/unit/test_i18n.py` fails
when a default Home text has no Persian.

## The font

`scripts/ci/build.ps1` downloads `Vazirmatn[wght].ttf` (version 33.003) and `OFL.txt` from
google/fonts at a pinned commit, checks the SHA-256 and bundles both in `app/ui/fonts`.
The font is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL.txt` ships next to it). In the
installed app both files are in `_internal/app/ui/fonts`; the build fails if they are missing.

## Check it on the PC (0.20.0)

1. Top bar > فارسی, close the app, open it again: Home is Persian and right to left.
2. A suggestion card, Details, Approve and Close now ask their questions in Persian.
3. Switch to Advanced: the pages are English, left to right. English in the top bar goes
   back after the next start.
